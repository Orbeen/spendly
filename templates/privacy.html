Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. What we collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to operate your account, display your expense history and summaries, and improve the reliability of the service. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data storage

Your information is stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the service.

4. Sharing your information

We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law or to operate core parts of the service (such as hosting infrastructure).

5. Your choices

You can review, update, or delete your account information at any time from your profile. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We'll note the "last updated" date above whenever changes are made.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
